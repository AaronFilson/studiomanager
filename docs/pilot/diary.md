# Before-and-after diary

_Part of the [pilot kit](README.md). The measures come from the research report's list of what a
pilot studio should measure ([report](../planning/reports/Pottery%20studio%20manager%20role.md#what-the-evidence-cant-tell-us-and-how-to-find-out))._

**Who fills it in:** the owner or manager, once a day, at closing. **Time:** under 3 minutes.
**How long:** week 0 (7 days, working as you do today, no app), then weeks 1–2 (14 days with the
app). Use the same rules all three weeks. Estimates rounded to 5 minutes are fine; a blank is better
than a guess.

## What to count

| Col | Measure | Count it like this |
|---|---|---|
| A | Admin minutes | Minutes today on paperwork and messages: firing slips and fees, member emails and texts, schedules and rosters, payments and payouts, stock counts and orders, maintenance records. **Not** loading kilns, teaching, cleaning, repairs or making. Includes E. Excludes filling in this diary |
| A2 | App setup minutes | Weeks 1–2 only. Minutes spent learning or setting up the app (adding members, fixing a fee rule). Kept apart so setup doesn't hide savings |
| B | "Is it ready?" contacts | Questions about whether a piece is fired or ready, by channel: **P** in person, **T** text or phone, **E** email, **S** social media or chat. Count questions, not people |
| C | Ready notices sent | For each batch you told a member was ready today: days from drop-off to the notice, and days from kiln unload to the notice. Write as `drop/unload`, e.g. `9/2`. Several batches: `9/2, 12/1` |
| D | Stock-outs | Items members or staff couldn't get today because they had run out (clay body, glaze, tool, consumable). Name them. A shortage you covered with a substitute counts; write "sub" |
| E | Payout reconciliation minutes | Minutes matching processor payouts or deposits (Square, Stripe, shop, marketplace) to sales. Blank on days you didn't |
| F | Maintenance done / missed | Tasks done today, and tasks that were due but not done (kiln checks, clay trap, wheel, extinguisher, cleaning rota). Write `done / missed`, e.g. `2 / 1` |
| G | Unusual today | One line: anything that makes today not typical. Holiday, sale, kiln down, staff off, class end, big firing |

Once a week, at the end of day 7, also fill in the weekly box.

## Comparing before and after

**Be honest about what this can show.** One studio, one baseline week, no control group, and a
diarist who knows what we hope to see. It is a case study. It can show a direction and its rough
size, and it can show that something is *not* working. It can't prove the app saves time in general.

**Which weeks to compare.** Week 1 carries setup and learning (column A2) and a novelty effect, so
compare **week 0 with week 2** first, and with the average of weeks 1–2 second. If the studio
will keep the diary for two baseline weeks instead of one, take it: one week can't show how much
an ordinary week varies.

**Write down the expected direction before week 1 starts** (for example: B down, C's
unload-to-notice days down, A flat or down). Then a result can disagree with us.

**Drop or flag unusual days.** Any day with a G note that changes the workload (a sale, a closure,
a kiln down) is shown in the write-up but left out of the main comparison, in both periods.

The thresholds below are our rules of thumb, chosen in advance. They aren't from the research.

| Measure | Meaningful if | Noise if |
|---|---|---|
| A admin minutes (weekly total) | Week 2 differs from week 0 by **20% or more**, weeks 1 and 2 move the same way (ignoring A2), and no G note explains it | Smaller, or the two after-weeks disagree |
| B "is it ready?" contacts (weekly total) | The difference between week 0 and week 2 is more than **2 × √(week 0 + week 2)**. These are counts of separate events, so their chance variation is about the square root of the count | Within that band. Example: 12 against 6 is a difference of 6 and the band is 2 × √18 ≈ 8.5, so noise. 20 against 6: difference 14, band ≈ 10.2, meaningful |
| C days to ready notice | The median unload-to-notice days drops by **1 day or more**, and at least 3 of every 4 after-batches beat the week-0 median | Fewer than 4 batches in either period: report the raw numbers only |
| D stock-outs | Not testable in three weeks (the report measures them per month). Report the raw list, and ask C4 | |
| E reconciliation minutes | Not testable unless a reconciliation happens in both periods. Report raw minutes per payout and ask C5 | |
| F maintenance done / missed | Report the raw done and missed counts per period. A drop in "missed" from several to zero is worth noting, but treat it as a story, not a measurement | |

**Cross-checks.** In weeks 1–2, the app's own records (ready notice times, the firing log,
maintenance tasks) can check columns C and F. Week 0 has only the diary. If the diary and the app
disagree, report both.

**In the write-up,** give each measure's raw numbers for both periods before any conclusion, say
which rule above it met, and quote the closing interview where it explains a number.
