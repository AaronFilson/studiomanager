# Studio Manager pilot: a one-page guide

**Studio Manager is a free studio manager that actually knows what a kiln is.** It keeps the back
room in one place, beside the booking or shop tool you already use. It doesn't replace that tool.

## What it does for you

- **Pieces:** each piece gets a photo and a stage, from greenware to pickup. Members can check
  their own pieces' status, so fewer "is it ready?" questions.
- **Kilns:** a queue per kiln, a firing log, and one "ready" email per drop-off, sent when all of a
  member's pieces are out, not one per kiln load.
- **Today view:** what is in each kiln, what is waiting, low stock, maintenance due and who is on,
  so whoever covers for you can run the day.
- **Calendar and rosters:** kiln and room bookings, monitor and work-exchange shifts with credited
  hours, and certifications that expire.
- **Money:** firing fees by your rule (per cubic inch, per pound or flat), balances, gift cards
  and firing cards. You enter each payout from Square, Stripe or your shop and match it to its
  sales. A summary export (CSV) for QuickBooks.
- **Clay and supplies:** par levels, low-stock alerts and purchase requests.
- **Maintenance:** recurring tasks for kilns, other equipment and the building, by days or by firings.
- **Phones:** the web app works on a phone. Members get emails.

**Not yet:** taking card payments, live QuickBooks posting, push notifications.

Free use of the app, during and after the pilot. Setup help. Your own before-and-after numbers.
A real say in what we build next.

## Your data

- **It is your data.** You can export all of your studio's data at any time.
- **Studios are kept apart in the database itself:** every row belongs to one studio, and the
  database refuses to show it to anyone else.
- **We delete your studio's data when you ask.**
- Members' email addresses are used only for invites and studio notices.

## First-day setup

1. **Create your studio** and choose US units.
2. **Add your kilns.** Optionally, set how many firings between maintenance checks.
3. **Paste your member emails** on the Members page (a spreadsheet column works). Each gets an invite.
4. **Set your fee rule** in Settings: unit, rate, minimum and rounding. Set your hold period for
   finished work.
5. Enter one real drop-off end to end, with a member watching.
