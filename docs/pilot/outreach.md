# Outreach: US studios to approach first

_Part of the [pilot kit](README.md). Every studio and fact below comes from the
[tools census in 03](../planning/03-tools-in-use.md#per-studio-table) or the
[research notes](../planning/research_notes/Pottery%20studio%20manager%20role/). Contact names,
emails and phone numbers aren't in the repo: TBD, from each studio's own site._

## How the list was chosen

From the 42 US studios in the census:

1. **The piece → kiln → pickup loop has to matter.** Membership or community studios, co-ops and
   nonprofits with an open studio. Left out for now: universities (4), museum and city art
   schools that publish little about operations, and PYOP (2 US; 03 calls PYOP a possible early
   segment, so it is a second batch).
2. **No pottery software or home-built app already.** Left out: Gasworks NYC, Ceramigos and John
   Burrison Pottery Studio (Kiln Fire); BKLYN CLAY (its own firing app); Still Life Ceramics (its
   own firing tracking system). Past Lives Makerspace in Portland built the same feature in its own
   app, so 03 rules it out.
3. **The audit could read the site** and the studio is open. Left out: MudFire and The Workshop
   Mpls (unreachable), Fancy Banana ("closing at the end of September").
4. **Visible evidence of the problems the app solves:** paper or form-based firing records, more
   than one tool taking money, monthly invoicing, or required studio clay.
5. **A spread of models,** because the research says who manages depends on the model: owner-run,
   hired manager, nonprofit, member-run co-op and town-run.

**Order.** Start with 1–4: membership studios where the decision probably sits with a founder (an
inference from their sites). Then 5–8, which have a named manager to talk to. Then 9 and 10, whose
yes probably needs members or a town.

**Where else owners talk.** r/PotteryBusiness and the Ceramic Arts Daily Business forum; r/Pottery
bans self-promotion [03]. Lead with free, data export and "works with your storefront", not a
feature list [03].

## First email

Plain text, no attachments. Fill the brackets from the table above; keep the reason specific and
under 15 words, so the email stays under 150.

> **Subject:** A free kiln and piece tracker: looking for one studio to try it
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm building Studio Manager, a free tool for community pottery studios. It tracks pieces from
> greenware to pickup, runs the kiln queue and firing log, and sends one "ready" email per
> drop-off. It works beside [their booking tool], not instead of it.
>
> I'm looking for one US studio to use it for two weeks and tell me plainly what helps and what
> doesn't. I picked [studio] because [one line from "Why it fits"].
>
> It would take about four to five hours over three weeks: two short interviews, a three-minute
> daily log, and setup, which I'd do with you. It stays free afterward, and your data stays yours to
> export or delete.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes for a call in the next two weeks?
>
> [sender name, phone, email: TBD]

## Follow-up

Send once, about a week later, as a reply to the first email. Then stop.

> Hi [name],
>
> Following up on my note about Studio Manager. If a pilot isn't a fit right now, I'd still value
> 15 minutes on how [studio] tracks firings and pickups today; it helps me build the right thing.
> And if someone else handles the studio's day-to-day, I'd be glad to talk to them instead.
>
> Thanks either way,
> [sender name]
